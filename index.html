About the Lab

Advancing robotics through research, collaboration, and teaching.

The FYUST Robotics Laboratory brings together faculty, graduate students, and undergraduate researchers to develop intelligent robotic systems that solve meaningful problems. Our work spans perception, motion planning, control, and human-robot interaction in both academic and applied settings.

Research Areas

What we study

Autonomous Systems

Navigation, localization, and planning for mobile robots operating in dynamic environments.

Computer Vision

Scene understanding, object recognition, and vision-based control for real-time applications.

Human-Robot Interaction

Safe and intuitive interfaces that support collaboration between people and machines.
